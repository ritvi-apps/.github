## Ritvi Apps

Small, focused mobile apps for India. React Native and Expo, with RevenueCat for
purchases and Firebase on the backend.

### On the Play Store

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[Charades — Act it Out!](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.riteshf.charadesbollywood)** | Offline party game. 5,149 cards across Bollywood, Tollywood, Hollywood and Family & Kids. |
| **[Tic Tac Toe — Classic XO](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.riteshf.tictactoe)** | The classic 3×3 game. Minimax AI that plays perfectly on Hard. |

### In progress

- **Aakalan** (आकलन) — expense tracking and bill splitting, settled over UPI.
- **Chitragupt** (चित्रगुप्त) — personal tax ledger for Indian filers.
- **AskCal** — AI calorie counter. Photograph a meal, then answer the one question the camera could not.
